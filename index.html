About me

Care that goes beyond symptoms

I am a physiotherapy graduate with a passion for musculoskeletal health, therapeutic massage, orthopedic rehabilitation and healthy aging.

I support people living with muscle tension, spasms, stiffness, mobility limits and the physical challenges that follow orthopedic injuries.

I believe rehabilitation should restore movement, build confidence and improve everyday quality of life. I'm also pursuing an MSc in Global Health Policy & Management at Brandeis University, bringing data and policy thinking into care.
